Who Is The Emergency Magician?

Travis Martois is a proud member of The Academy of Magical Arts at the World Famous Magic Castle in Hollywood and The Magic Circle in London. He has been a Featured performer in the Close-Up Gallery at the Magic Castle.

By day, he is a Physician Assistant with over 35 years in emergency medicine — starting as an EMT at age 19. By night, he brings magic that heals the soul.
